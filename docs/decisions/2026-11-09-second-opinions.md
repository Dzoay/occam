# The second opinions

**Decided by the user, 2026-11-09:** "Yes, both, as second opinions".

## What it means in practice

`arris` and `solidcore` may be cloned, built and run outside the workspace at pinned commits, as second opinions beside
the [OCCT oracle](2026-11-09-occt-oracle.md). They are driven only through their documented interfaces, and their source
is not read, under [the provenance rules](2026-11-08-licence-and-provenance.md). `arris` test cases join the corpus only
with a licence and origin check per case, and any of unclear origin stay out. `solidcore` cases stay external, as
[the census plan](../plan/census.md) says.
